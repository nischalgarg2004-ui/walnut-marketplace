# Card sort & interview template (Part A4)

Use 8–12 cards with these **working labels** (adjust after prep):

**Queues / objects:** Opportunities, Applications, Deals, Deliverables, Payouts, Campaigns, Earnings, Profile, Settings, Notifications, Help.

## Card sort (15 min)

1. **Open sort:** group cards however it makes sense; name each group.
2. **Compare** to proposed sidebar IA; note mismatches.

## Interview prompts (10 min each)

1. Walk through last time you **applied** to a brand campaign—where would you look for status?
2. Where do you expect **money** and **deadline** to appear?
3. What does **“deal”** mean to you vs **“application”**?

## Recording

- Date, role (creator/brand), optional anonymized ID.
- Top 3 friction quotes.
- Recommended label changes → update `NAVIGATION.md`.
